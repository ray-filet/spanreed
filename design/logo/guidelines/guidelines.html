Door Ajar

An arched door left ajar at home. The sliver of warm light at its latch edge is also the text cursor: someone is home, and the note is open.

2Clear space

R = half the door's width (its arch radius). Keep the zone empty: 1.5 R around the horizontal lockup (= one x-height), 1 R around the stacked lockup and the symbol, one x-height (the height of the n) around the wordmark and the app-icon lockup. It scales with the logo.
