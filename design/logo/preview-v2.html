  • Is the idea still clear at 16–24 px? Which details disappear first?
  • Does the silhouette survive the squint (blur) test?
  • Does the one-colour black version still carry the concept?
  • Does the reversed (white on dark) version look heavier? (irradiation → thin it)
  • Any unintended readings when mirrored or rotated 180°?
  • Does it hold up on the brand colour, a photo and a pattern?
  • In the favicon/app-icon tests, is the symbol optically sized and centred?
  • On the shelf: does it stand out from the references, or blend in?
  • Greyscale: do colour segments still separate by value?
  • Would you recognise it again tomorrow from memory?
